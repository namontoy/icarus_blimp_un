# Rope rig, 2026-10-30: the frame, the signs, the gondola's yaw kick and the first holds

**Rig:** the gondola without the envelope, hung from the ceiling by a rope through a swivel,
battery on board. **Firmware:** `i2c_tools` at `2ea7820` (the `param` build). **Ground:** the
Windows laptop on Ethernet for the internet and on `ICARUS-EB75ED` for the blimp, so the
commands ran from the coding session while the team did the physical steps. The captures are
in the gitignored `captures/` (`rig-imu-signs`, `rig-motor-signs`); the analysis scripts were
session scratch. What it fed: `CONFIG_CTL_IMU_AXES`, `CONFIG_CTL_MAG_AXES`,
`CONFIG_MOTOR_POSITIONS` and `CONFIG_MOTOR_SIGNS` (branch `control-inc3`).

## The team's conventions, from their photo

Forward is toward the forward camera (the nose). The team calls the outer motor on the left of
the photo, which was taken from in front of and above the nose, "FL". Seen from the front, the
blimp's own left is the photo's right, which is what the measurements below confirmed.

## Measured

**Motor identity.** `blimp_link.py motor` at 0.25 for 3 s on FL, RL, FR, RR in turn: each
command turned the motor the team expected for that name (checked by eye), and the readback
matched every command.

**Axes**, one 73 s capture with the gondola hung still, then by hand: nose up about 30°, the
FL/RL ("blue") side up about 30°, a counter-clockwise turn (seen from above) of about 90°. The
gyro's rotation vectors and the accelerometer agree:

| Move | Gyro rotation axis (IMU) | Accelerometer change | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nose up, 34° | −x | y −0.59 g | right = IMU −x, so forward = IMU −y |
| FL side up, 27° | +y (−0.41 x) | x −0.42 g | a rotation about −forward: the **right** side going up |
| CCW turn, 93° | +z | — | up = IMU +z; the accelerometer at rest agrees (cos 0.99) |

So: forward = −IMU y, left = +IMU x, up = +IMU z (`"-Y +X +Z"`), and **the motors named FL and
RL are on the blimp's right**, mirrored against `core/`'s frame. The yaw rate is positive
counter-clockwise, as `core/` expects. The gondola hangs about 6° nose-down on the rope.

**Magnetometer axes**, fitted to the same capture: of the 48 signed axis permutations, the gyro's
integrated attitude fits best (0.012 G rms) with the QMC5883L's x, y, z already along forward,
left, up, with a hard-iron offset of about −0.18 G along the IMU's y; the mirror image fits
equally and is rejected because it needs a left-handed chip. Field magnitude after the offset
0.23 G. Not a calibration: about 90° of yaw and 30° of tilt only.

**Push directions**, one capture, each motor at +0.2 then −0.2 for 2 s, the gondola free on the
swivel. FL +: the yaw rate rose at +248 °/s², counter-clockwise; FR +: −227 °/s², clockwise;
each minus reversed it. With FL on the right and FR on the left, + pushes forward on both. RL
and RR twist the gondola in opposite directions (+52 and −53 °/s² on +), so the heave propellers
counter-rotate; by hand under each inner propeller the team felt the air pushed down on + and up
on −: + lifts on both.

**The front pair's arm**: 24 cm between the FL and FR shafts, so l_f = 0.12 m (`params.c` and the
sim scene carry 0.10; AGENTS.md said ±20 cm).

**The gondola's yaw inertia** from FL's kick: 248 °/s² (4.3 rad/s²) at 0.2 duty, where the
2026-10-28 scale curves give about 0.5 gf a motor, on the 0.12 m arm: about 1.4e-4 kg·m², some
1/60 of the 0.0083 kg·m² `params.c` estimates with the envelope. The thrust at 0.2 duty is the
weak link of that number (FL was never on the scale). The swivel barely damps it: the gyro
saturated at its ±250 °/s range during these 2 s pulses, and a spin lost two thirds of its rate
in about 5 s.

**Battery** (`battery`): 3.770 V, then 3.755 V on a second cell (1877 mV at GPIO1 times the
nominal 2.000). Not checked against a meter.

## The first closed-loop run (B1, the bench hold, firmware `ea9696c`)

`hold_test.py --steps 0 30 0 --dwell 10`, props on, `yaw.kp` = `yaw.kd` = 0.0006, torque cap
±0.0012 N·m, `motor_max_thrust_n` 0.088 N, deadband 10 %. The hold armed (the heading estimate
reset to 0 at the start) and its error followed the setpoint (0.52 rad at the step), but every
motor stayed at 0.000 for the 30 s, the capture's wrench was zero throughout, and the gondola
only made its own ±2° torsional swing on the rope. Cause: `core/`'s mixer zeroed any front-pair
difference under a fixed 0.5 gf, and the loop asked for 0.26 gf (docs/10). Two earlier attempts
did not start: the laptop had lost the blimp's Wi-Fi after a battery change, and then the
estimator's 60 s stillness count kept restarting because the gondola was being held (the ToF read
13–20 mm, a hand under it); `hold_test.py` now waits for the count instead of refusing.

The team also set the motors' working limit: 30 % duty at most, to spare the electronics; the
scale sheet of 2026-10-28 gives 0.5–2 gf a motor there. They remembered about 9 gf; the sheet has
that near 75 % duty, so a new measurement of FL and FR at 30 % is the next number to take.

## B1 to B3: the hold turning the gondola (firmware `556bd07`, evening)

Common to the three: `yaw.kp` = `yaw.kd` = 0.0006 (N·m/rad, N·m·s/rad), torque cap ±0.00118 N·m,
`motor_max_thrust_n` 0.0098 N (about 1 gf, the 30 % ceiling's thrust on the 2026-10-28 sheet),
`front_half_span_m` 0.12, the driver's deadband 10 % and ceiling 30 %; steps 0°, +30°, 0°,
10 s each; the gondola free on the swivel, hands off, battery 3.75 V. Captures
`hold-heading-20261030-215312` (B1), `-215905` (B2) and `-220218` (B3).

| Run | What changed | Step to +30° | Back to 0° | Holding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | nothing (the deadband param and the ceiling) | inside 5° after 3.2 s, no overshoot, final +2.5° ± 1.1° | inside 5° after 1.4 s, overshoot 4.3°, final +1.5° ± 1.0° | a 0.88 s cycle, ±12–15 °/s, about ±2° |
| B2 | `hold surge` 0.5 gf | the error stays near 5° | inside 5° after 1.3 s, final −2.2° ± 0.8° | no cycle (±2 °/s), but parked about 5° off |
| B3 | B2 and `front_deadband_n` 0.0001 N (0.01 gf) | the error 2.6–3.5°, growing | inside 5° after 1.4 s, overshoot 1.1°, final +1.0° ± 0.2° | ±0.5 °/s; at 0° within 1.1° |

Measured along the way:

- **The signs end to end.** At +30° the motor the team calls FL (blue, on the blimp's right)
  pushed and the nose turned left (counter-clockwise), the first closed-loop check of the frame,
  the axis maps and the motor map together. The heading `core/` estimates follows the integrated
  gyro to within a constant offset (the origin set at arming) and a slow 0.3 °/s drift.
- **The motors lag.** After B1's step command (10.00 s) the yaw rate starts to move at
  10.08–10.10 s, so about 80–100 ms to start pushing; after the loop cut that motor (10.32 s)
  the turn kept speeding up until 10.54 s, though the other motor was braking from 10.40 s: a
  coasting motor keeps pushing for about 0.2 s. Without a common push only one front motor runs
  at a time, every reversal stops one and starts the other, and on 1/60 of the blimp's inertia
  that lag is B1's 0.88 s cycle (the loop's own derivative term drives most of it: kd·rate is
  1.3e-4 N·m at 12 °/s, against 2e-5 from kp at 2°).
- **A common push removes the cycle** (B2): with 0.5 gf of surge both front motors run at
  about 0.25 gf and a correction speeds one up and slows the other. It leaves the mixer's
  cut-off as a dead zone: 0.0005 N on the 0.12 m arm against kp 0.0006 is 5.7°, and B2 parked
  at its edge; at 0.0001 N (B3) the hold sits within about 1°. With the motors already turning,
  the cut-off has nothing left to protect.
- **The rope twists back.** B3 at +30° drifted from 27.4° to 26.5° over 6 s with the error
  growing: a restoring torque of about 3e-5 N·m (the loop's FL–FR difference, 0.03 throttle)
  from the twisted rope. Proportional gain alone leaves that error; the integral term is B4.
- **The compass** (raw x, y, uncalibrated) read about 7° where the gyro said 21°: a hard-iron
  offset as large as the field (above) compresses the angle. The estimator's heading did not
  follow it; the compass calibration stays with the envelope session.

## Two `core/` bugs the runs exposed (fixed in `core/` 0.2.1, not yet flashed)

- **The arming tick steered on the old heading.** `blimp_estimator_update()` writes the tick's
  state before arming calls `blimp_estimator_zero()`, so the first hold tick saw the heading from
  before the origin moved: one tick at 0.065 throttle from −10° (B1), one at the torque cap from
  −125° (B2). Too short for the motor to answer (80 ms to start), but a kick on the blimp too.
  `blimp.c` now refreshes the state after the zero (`blimp_estimator_output()`).
- **Single ticks fell into failsafe.** The S3 stamps a ground packet on its link task and the
  tick at its start; a packet landing in between is a few ms newer than the tick, and `core/`'s
  unsigned link age wrapped to about 71 minutes: zero output, every PID reset and guidance in
  FAILSAFE for that tick. 21 such ticks in B2's 1500, 10 in B3's (two at the +30° step, which
  broke that step's metrics), often in pairs 0.1 s apart. In a race it would drop guidance into
  FAILSAFE, and it would empty an integral term before it could act. `core/` now takes a packet
  up to 100 ms newer than the tick as fresh; further ahead is a broken clock and stays stale.

Both reproduced first by new checks in `core/tests/test_blimp.c` (six failures on 0.2.0, none on
0.2.1); `tools/verify.py --full` 13/13; the simulator sweep (Windows, seeds 0–4, 220 s) completes
the same loops with the same failsafe counts, the sensorless rows identical run for run, the ToF
rows' best laps moved (22.6–25.4 s and 20.5–25.6 s, from 21.6–24.0 and 23.0–24.2) by the altitude
the arming tick now reports. `hold_test.py` gained `--surge`.

**Next on the rig:** flash 0.2.1, then B4: B3 with `yaw.ki` 0.0002 and the integral clamped to
±0.0003 N·m, 15 s per step; pass is a final error under 1° at +30°.

## Seen on the way, not chased

- The barometer: about one BME280 reading in thirteen (every ~3.4 s) comes back 1–10 °C cold
  with its pressure 150–1440 Pa low, a jump of up to 120 m; it is also in the capture taken that
  morning from the ground station. The bench hold withholds the barometer from `core/` until the
  read is fixed (docs/10).
- Two restarts of the blimp in the session; the second was a battery change, the first was not
  explained.
